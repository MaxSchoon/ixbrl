## What changed

### Jurisdictions

- **SEC EDGAR** (`references/jurisdictions/sec-edgar.md`): EDGAR Filer
  Manual Volume II **Version 78**, deployed 14 September 2026 with Release
  26.3 (adopting release 33-11438), is current. Release 26.3 **removed every
  2024 SEC taxonomy**, quarterly releases included; the 2024 row stays, now
  marked removed. `edgartaxonomies.xml` v78 lists IFRS 2025 only (v77 still
  listed 2021, 2022, 2024 and 2025). New rows: FND 2026q3 (accepted at 26.3),
  the proposed 2027 GRT and SRT (comments to 30 October 2026) and the draft
  2027 SEC taxonomies (feedback to 16 November 2026), both not accepted. The
  14 September 2026 order relieving five market-intermediary forms from
  Inline XBRL (Exhibits H and I excepted) and the FDTA joint data standards
  (effective 1 October 2026, no reporting change on their own) are recorded.
- **EBA** (`references/dpm.md`): the 4.3 package is downloadable (July
  2026); the **4.4 phase 1** final technical package was published on
  12 October 2026, first reference dates 31 December 2026 and 31 March 2027;
  4.4.1 is announced for early 2027. EIOPA: no new version.
- **ESEF and IFRS** (`references/esef.md`): IFRS 18 is endorsed by
  Commission Regulation (EU) 2026/338 of 13 February 2026. No new ESEF
  taxonomy, RTS or Reporting Manual; the IFRS Foundation's 2026 proposed
  updates are proposals.
- **UK** (`references/jurisdictions/uk-frc.md`): the FRC's draft 2027 suite
  (consultation closed 20 September 2026, final aimed at November 2026) is
  a new draft row; the FCA's NSM Technical Guidelines are at V11.0 (August
  2026). Companies House software-only filing stays at April 2028.
- **Netherlands** (`references/jurisdictions/nl-sbr.md`): the KVK Inline
  XBRL 2026 taxonomy has its own release-calendar row (definitive
  21 December, production 31 December 2026, status subject to reservation);
  the skill had given it NT21's dates.
- **Denmark** (`references/jurisdictions/dk-erst.md`): Erhvervsstyrelsen
  implemented ESEF 2025 on 26 August 2026, for periods from 1 January 2026.
- **No change found**: Finland (no newer package), Germany (6.10 stands, no
  7.0), Belgium (26.0.15 stands), France (no newer AMF instruction),
  EIOPA, ESAP formats.

### New mandates

- **Hong Kong** (new file `references/jurisdictions/hk-ird.md`): Profits
  Tax e-filing with iXBRL financial statements and tax computations is
  mandatory for in-scope multinational groups from year of assessment
  2025/26, with transitional paper exceptions. Added to the reference
  index, the description's trigger terms, `references/taxonomies.md` and
  the source registry.
- **Recorded as drafts, not mandates**: Japan's draft 2027 EDINET taxonomy
  (11 September 2026, adds a sustainability taxonomy); EFRAG's draft XBRL
  taxonomy for the revised ESRS (17 September 2026; sustainability mark-up
  stays suspended).
- **Not confirmed, so not recorded**: an Irish CRO iXBRL procurement (the
  minutes were not readable) and a Finnish committee report on the
  Accounting Act bill (search extract only). Both are in the registry for
  the next cycle.

## Skill-craft verdicts

| Practice | Source | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Write evaluations before more documentation; measure with and without the skill | skill authoring best practices; skill-creator; arXiv 2610.08875 | **adopted** as a deterministic eval in CI (`tests/eval_skill.py`); a model-graded with/without comparison is **rejected for the gate**, because a gate must give the same answer twice and run without credentials |
| Trigger sets with near-miss negatives and a fixed held-out split | agentskills.io, optimizing descriptions; skill-creator | **adopted**: trigger and negative cases in both splits |
| Topic-disjoint, sealed held-out set; optimisers leak training answers | arXiv 2610.12601 | **adapted**: held-out cases are phrasing-disjoint, append-only and digest-frozen, and a held-out query copied into the skill fails the eval; a file-disjoint split is not used, because every file must stay routable |
| Do not collapse grading to one scalar; guard unscored requirements | arXiv 2610.13287 | **adopted**: five metrics per split, each with its own bar, plus floors (size, description length, vintages rows kept) |
| Harness integrity: a changed grader stops the loop until reviewed | claude-api `shared/evals/eval-hillclimb.md` | **adopted**: the eval digests itself and its distractors |
| Declare runtime needs in `compatibility` | agentskills.io specification | **adopted** in the SKILL.md frontmatter |
| A should-not boundary in the description | Codex skills guidance; agentskills.io | **rejected this cycle**: the lexical eval cannot score negation, and a change it cannot measure is not a hillclimb step |

**Held-out delta for the cycle**: trigger recall 0.875 → 1.000, route hit@1
unchanged at 0.9167, route MRR 0.9514 → 0.9479 (the new jurisdiction row;
inside the declared slack), specificity and facts unchanged at 1.000.

The eval harness changed once during the cycle, after review: bars are now
measured on the case set they were frozen with, so adding a hard train case
cannot move an old bar. The freeze history in `evals/baseline.json` records
both freezes.
