  on 23 October 2026 the government **paused** the small and micro P&L filing
  requirement and kept abridged accounts and filleting; the software-only iXBRL
  mandate from April 2028 "is going ahead as planned". The 9 June story the skill
  cited is withdrawn. Overseas companies can file iXBRL package accounts online
  (guidance update of 25 September 2026).
- **Netherlands** (`references/jurisdictions/nl-sbr.md`): the alfa of KVK
  taxonomy 2026 is served at `kvk/2026-12-31.a/` (notices of 22 and 29 October;
  the first combined ZIP was withdrawn); unsuffixed entry points still answer 404.

  Report Package limit from 100 MB to 50 MB, not yet in the RTS or Reporting
  Manual. `NL-INLINE-2026` is still not in Arelle 2.47.4.
- **Netherlands, unresolved:** the FAQ of 28 September 2026 changes one question,
  2.3.7, adding "The annual report must be filed by email in PDF format" beside
  unchanged text saying an ESEF report can be filed via SBR. Recorded as open in
  the skill, in both directions.
- **SEC** (`references/jurisdictions/sec-edgar.md`, `references/taxonomies.md`):
  no release after 26.3 and no Filer Manual after Version 78. New proposal rows:

  full taxonomy was re-uploaded on 23 October; the IRRD row now has its reference
  dates from EIOPA's roadmap. A stale paragraph in `references/taxonomies.md`
  that still called 4.3 and 4.4 "expected" now agrees with `references/dpm.md`.
- **ESEF** (`references/esef.md`): new § 8.1 on ESMA's 2026 enforcement priority,

  October cycle's unconfirmed procurement is a funding application, no filer duty.

  2026) and the Voluntary Standard (2026/1560) change the standards. Sustainability
  mark-up stays suspended.

### A gate defect fixed

`tests/test_check_sources.py` judged the registry against a fixed 2026-10-13, so
setting `checked_on` to any later date, as this routine requires, failed three
tests. It now takes its date from the registry's latest `checked_on`, still
without the clock, and the ahead-of-today case is relative to it. Every assertion
stays. With the future-date check removed from a temporary copy,
`test_dates_must_be_real_and_not_ahead` fails, so the test still bites.
